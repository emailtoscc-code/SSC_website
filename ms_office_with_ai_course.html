Course Objective

Practical, job-oriented Office + AI training

This course develops practical MS Office skills combined with modern AI tools. Students learn to create professional documents, analyze data, build presentations, automate repetitive work, and use AI responsibly for workplace productivity.

Course Modules

Click any module to expand or collapse the topics

Module 1 — MS Word + AI +
  • Document creation, formatting, styles, themes and templates
  • Page setup, sections, headers, footers and page numbering
  • Tables, images, shapes, icons and SmartArt
  • Mail Merge, labels and envelopes
  • Table of Contents, footnotes, citations and captions
  • Track Changes, comments and document protection
  • Professional letters, reports, resumes, notices and certificates
  • AI for drafting, rewriting, summarizing and proofreading
  • AI prompts for official letters, reports and professional content
Module 2 — MS Excel + AI +
  • Data entry, formatting, tables, sorting and filtering
  • SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT and COUNTA
  • IF, AND, OR and IFERROR
  • SUMIF, SUMIFS, COUNTIF, COUNTIFS and AVERAGEIF
  • XLOOKUP, VLOOKUP and INDEX-MATCH
  • Text, date and time functions
  • Data validation, conditional formatting and data cleaning
  • Charts, PivotTables, PivotCharts and dashboards
  • AI for formulas, analysis, summaries, trends and chart suggestions
Module 3 — PowerPoint + AI +
  • Slides, layouts, themes and Slide Master
  • Professional slide design and visual hierarchy
  • Images, icons, shapes, SmartArt, charts and tables
  • Transitions, animations and presenter tools
  • Speaker notes and presentation delivery
  • Business, training, project and seminar presentations
  • AI for presentation ideas, outlines, slide content and speaker notes
  • AI-assisted rewriting, summaries and storytelling
Module 4 — AI Tools for Office Work +
  • Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs)
  • Prompt Engineering: role, context, task, constraints and output format
  • ChatGPT for office productivity
  • Google Gemini for writing, brainstorming and productivity
  • Microsoft Copilot for Microsoft 365 workflows
  • AI for document writing, proofreading and summarization
  • AI for spreadsheets, formulas and data analysis
  • AI for presentations and visual content
  • AI for emails, official messages, research and meeting summaries
  • Responsible AI: privacy, fact-checking, copyright and sensitive-data awareness
Module 5 — Practical Projects +
  • Professional office report in MS Word using AI
  • Excel sales/employee/student dataset analysis
  • Excel dashboard using PivotTable and charts
  • Professional PowerPoint presentation using AI
  • Official letter/document workflow using AI
  • Final integrated Word + Excel + PowerPoint + AI productivity project
